Your Personalized Recommendations

You're operating at a strong level of alignment overall, with particularly solid grounding in your sense of self and your beliefs about who you are. Your future orientation is especially robust, suggesting you have clear vision and direction for where you're headed. At the same time, there's a noticeable gap in how you're showing up in your relationships and expressing your authentic self with others, which stands in contrast to the clarity you have internally.

Your lowest score in Relationships and Authenticity is actually where your greatest growth opportunity lies. Research in social psychology consistently shows that authentic connection with others isn't just about feeling good—it's a primary driver of sustained wellbeing, resilience, and even professional effectiveness. When there's a disconnect between our internal clarity and how we show up with others, it creates an invisible drain on our energy and limits our impact. Given your dual leadership roles spanning nonprofit vision work and corporate risk management, this gap may be costing you more than you realize in terms of influence, team cohesion, and personal sustainability.

Given the complexity of balancing nonprofit leadership with corporate compliance responsibilities, working with a coach or therapist who understands organizational dynamics could help you navigate the specific tensions between these worlds. They can provide a structured space to explore how you're managing competing identities and expectations across such different environments.

Schedule three specific one-on-one conversations this month with people in different spheres of your life—one from your nonprofit work, one from banking, one personal—where your only agenda is to share something true about what you're actually experiencing right now, not what you think they need to hear.

Identify one recurring meeting or interaction where you typically perform a role rather than show up as yourself, and experiment with saying one honest thing you'd normally edit out, even if it's as simple as admitting uncertainty or acknowledging difficulty.

Create a daily five-minute practice where you write down one moment from that day when you held back from expressing something authentic, and name specifically what you were protecting yourself from.

Set a boundary with yourself about code-switching between your two professional worlds by choosing one value or communication style that you'll keep consistent across both environments for the next two weeks, regardless of context.

Find one person in your life who already knows the unfiltered version of you and ask them to tell you what they notice about how you're different when you're with them versus how you seem to show up elsewhere.

Recommended Resources

Books

Set Boundaries, Find Peace by Nedra Glover Tawwab — This will help you move from managing perceptions to building genuine connections by learning how to protect your energy while staying present in relationships.

The Gifts of Imperfection by Brené Brown — Directly addresses the exhaustion that comes from performing instead of connecting, offering a framework for showing up authentically without the weight of perfectionism.

Practices

Daily Energy Mapping — At the end of each day, note three moments when you felt energized and three when you felt drained, looking for patterns in what actually fuels or depletes you rather than what you think should.

The One Honest Thing — Choose one safe relationship and practice sharing one unfiltered thought or feeling per week, starting small with low-stakes truths to build your capacity for being seen.
