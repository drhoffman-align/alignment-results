Your Personalized Recommendations

Your scores reveal someone who has done meaningful work on their past and is building toward a future vision, yet you're experiencing some friction in the present moment. With strong resolution around what's behind you and solid forward momentum, the gap appears in how fully you're inhabiting your current reality and expressing who you are right now in your daily life and relationships.

Your lowest scores in self-acceptance, core beliefs, and authentic relating aren't weaknesses to fix—they're the precise leverage points where small shifts will unlock the momentum you've already built. Research in identity development shows that our capacity to move forward is often constrained not by our goals or our past, but by how clearly we know and accept ourselves in the present. When you're uncertain about your core identity or holding back in relationships, it creates an invisible drag on everything else, even when you're otherwise capable and forward-moving.

As a consultant, you likely navigate complex interpersonal dynamics and shifting contexts regularly, which can make it harder to maintain a stable sense of self across different client environments and professional demands. This variability makes the work of anchoring into your core identity and practicing authenticity even more valuable as a foundation for sustainable success.

Start each morning by writing three sentences about who you are independent of your roles, using "I am" statements that have nothing to do with what you do or who you serve.

Schedule one conversation this week where you share something true about your internal experience that you would normally keep to yourself, choosing a relationship where there's already some trust.

Identify one belief you hold about yourself that you inherited or adopted without examination, write it down, and then write what you actually believe now based on your own experience.

Create a physical or digital space where you collect evidence of your actual qualities and capabilities, adding to it weekly with specific examples of when you showed up as yourself rather than as who you thought you should be.

Set a daily reminder to pause and notice one moment where you feel aligned between your internal state and your external expression, building your awareness of what authenticity actually feels like in your body.

Recommended Resources

Books

Get Good with Money by Tiffany Aliche — A practical, step-by-step approach to creating financial clarity and reducing money anxiety through simple systems that build confidence and stability.

Practices

Energy Audit — Track your energy levels at three points each day for one week, noting what activities drain or restore you, to identify your natural rhythm and design habits that fit your actual capacity.

Money Visibility Practice — Spend 15 minutes each Sunday reviewing where your money went that week without judgment, simply building awareness before making any changes to your spending patterns.
